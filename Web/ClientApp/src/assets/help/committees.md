# Committees

Manage each committee's public listing (description, members, photos) and its email forwarding.

## Members

Expand a committee to edit it:

- **Add member** and pick the resident from the search list - every member row must have a resident selected before Save works.
- Each member can have a title, bio, and photo (with a slider to adjust the photo's framing).
- Drag rows to control the order members are listed publicly.
- **Remove is permanent** for a saved member - you will be asked to confirm.

## Mail forwarding

Each committee can have mail to its committee address forwarded to members:

- The **"Receives forwarded email"** checkbox on a member is what routes committee mail to them - unchecking it quietly stops their forwards, so check it for at least one active member.
- The **Sender filter** controls who gets through automatically: **Directory only** forwards mail from known residents and holds everything else for review in [Approvals](#topic:approvals); **All senders** forwards everything.
- "Last polled" shows when the mailbox was last checked and whether it succeeded.

A badge shows how many of the committee's emails are waiting for approval, with a link to the [Approvals](#topic:approvals) queue.

## Committee order (Administrators)

Administrators can reorder committees with the arrows and **Save Order**. Saving the order re-saves every committee, so it is blocked while any committee has an incomplete member row.
