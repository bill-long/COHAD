# News

Write and manage the news posts shown on the public News page.

## Creating a post

**Create post** opens the editor:

- **Title** and an optional **excerpt** (auto-generated from the content if left blank; shown on news cards).
- **Post as** - publish under your own name or a committee, when your roles allow more than one.
- **Publish date and time** - posts dated in the future do not appear until that time, so you can schedule a post by setting a future date.
- **Content** - use the Visual editor, or switch to Markdown with the toggle; both edit the same content.
- **Featured image** - shown on news cards and in social-media link previews.

## Managing posts

- **View** opens the public post in a new tab.
- **Edit** reopens the editor.
- **Delete** permanently removes the post after confirmation - this cannot be undone.

Signed-in residents can comment on posts; comment authors and administrators can delete comments from the post page itself.
