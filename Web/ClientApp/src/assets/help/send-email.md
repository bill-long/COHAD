# Email

Compose and send email to the neighborhood from a committee mailbox, and review past and in-progress email jobs.

## Who can send

Reaching this page and sending are different permissions. Sending is granted **per committee mailbox** - the From list shows only the mailboxes your committee roles allow (Board, Welcome Committee, Garden Club, Social Committee, Sunshine Committee). An Administrator with no committee role, or a committee without a mailbox, can review jobs here but cannot send.

## Sending well

1. Pick the **From** mailbox, write the subject and body.
2. **Send a test email first.** Tests go only to the listed test recipients and run as a separate job, so the form stays editable while you check the result in your inbox.
3. When it looks right, **Send Email To Neighborhood**.

> **There is no confirmation dialog.** Send Email To Neighborhood queues the mailing to the entire matching audience immediately. If you sent by mistake, open the job and use **Cancel job** - it stops recipients not yet sent, but anything already sent is gone.

## After sending

Delivery runs in the background; progress updates live on this page and in the jobs list below. Click any job for its [details](#topic:email-jobs), including per-recipient delivery status.

Recipients on the [suppression list](#topic:suppressions) are skipped automatically and counted as "suppressed" - that is the system working as intended, not a failure. A recipient can also unsubscribe from a category of mail via the footer link in every message; those preferences are honored automatically.

## Reading the jobs list

- **From / To** - for a normal send, the committee mailbox and the audience. For a **committee forward**, From is the original sender and To is the committee mailbox, with a separate "Forwarded to" row for who received it.
- **Status** - Queued, In Progress, Completed, Partial (some recipients failed), Failed, or Cancelled.
