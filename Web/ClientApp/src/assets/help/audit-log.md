# Audit Log

A read-only history of administrative actions: who changed what, and when. Use it to answer "who edited this home?" or "when did this account's roles change?".

## Using it

- The search box matches the **user** who acted, the **subject** that was changed, and the **action** performed.
- Entries load newest first; keep scrolling to load older history until "End of audit history."

Each entry shows the time, the subject (what was acted on), the action, and the signed-in user who did it. Entries are written by the system as changes happen and cannot be edited or deleted from here.
