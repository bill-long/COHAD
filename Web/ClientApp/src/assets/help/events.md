# Events

Create and manage neighborhood events, and see who has signed up.

## Creating an event

**Create event** opens the editor:

- **Title**, **date and time**, and a **description** (Visual or Markdown editing - both edit the same content).
- **Allow signups** - lets residents register from the public Events page.
- **Signup type** - what a registration collects: adults and children, household only, children only, adults only, or a simple people count.
- **Promotional media** - a flyer image shown on the Events page and in social-media link previews.

## Signups

Each event with registrations shows its signup list: who registered, their counts, named adults/children, email, and when they signed up. Totals adapt to the signup type. If you close signups later, the existing list remains visible.

## Things to know

- **Delete is permanent** and discards the event's signup list with it - you will be asked to confirm.
- Past events move to their own section automatically; their signup lists remain available.
