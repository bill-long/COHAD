Dues

COHAD is a voluntary HOA that relies on residents who choose to pay dues, which are currently set at ${{baseDuesAmount}} per year. If you've paid dues in the past, we sincerely thank you for your help.

The majority of dues are used to maintain the beauty of our entrances, including irrigation, maintenance of grass and flower beds, new plants and shrubs as needed, and lighting. The hard work of the Garden Club and other volunteers helps to keep these costs down, but water and electricity bills must still be paid, and some contract work is necessary.

Dues also go towards community development for our neighborhood. We help the Social Committee host events like the Easter Egg Hunt, the ice cream truck after the Fourth of July Parade, block parties, and Breakfast with Santa. We also sponsor events like the annual garage sale and the Earth Day Clean Out.

If you have any questions about the budget, please contact the COHAD Board at board@cohad.org, or attend our annual meeting, where we discuss it in detail.
