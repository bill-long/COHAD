Who We Are

COHAD is the Canyon Oaks Homeowners Association in Denton, Texas. We are a voluntary HOA that brings neighbors together, maintains our community spaces, and sponsors events throughout the year.

Canyon Oaks is a welcoming neighborhood where residents look out for one another. Whether you've lived here for decades or just moved in, COHAD helps you stay connected and informed about what's happening in our community.

What We Do

The majority of our efforts go toward maintaining the beauty of our neighborhood entrances, including irrigation, maintenance of grass and flower beds, new plants and shrubs as needed, and lighting. The hard work of the Garden Club and other volunteers helps keep these costs down, but water and electricity bills must still be paid, and some contract work is necessary.

We also sponsor community events like the Easter Egg Hunt, the ice cream truck after the Fourth of July Parade, block parties, and Breakfast with Santa. We organize the annual garage sale and the Earth Day Clean Out, bringing neighbors together throughout the year.

Community Structure

Canyon Oaks has three parts — Canyon Oaks I, Canyon Oaks II Phase I, and Canyon Oaks II Phase II — each defined by their recorded plats.

The three main plat documents can be found on Google Driveopen_in_new. They are also available on the Denton Countyopen_in_new website.

Covenants & Architecture

Canyon Oaks has CC&Rs (Covenants, Conditions & Restrictions) that contain rules regarding new structures and modifications to existing structures. They can be found on Google Driveopen_in_new or through Denton County public recordsopen_in_new.

Article Two of the Covenants establishes an Architectural Committee. Approval of plans from this committee is required for new structures and modifications to be in compliance. For help navigating these rules, please consult the Property Addition Checklistopen_in_new prepared by the Architectural Committee.

Article Six establishes that enforcement of the Covenants is left to homeowners themselves, via the courts.

Voluntary HOA

Canyon Oaks has a voluntary HOA. The HOA uses voluntary dues (currently $225 per year) to maintain the entrances and host neighborhood events throughout the year.

As noted above, the voluntary HOA has no role in enforcing the Covenants. Enforcement is left to homeowners, per Article Six.
